# Candidate Landmark: Operational Picture

## What the Landmark Is

Inference: this candidate landmark groups the experience of understanding several projects quickly: changes since the last check, ongoing work, wider service status, and evidence of movement toward each destination. The homepage and progress decisions give this grouping a foundation; a specific layout and technical observation mechanism have not been chosen.

This is a provisional grouping, not a confirmed landmark assignment. The room has no configured Landmark Tracker collection; this Design Concept article is the fallback permitted by [CIC Blueprint Teardown — S-000B](../../specs/S-000B-cic-blueprint-teardown/SPEC.md). No native landmark or question-card record is claimed.
