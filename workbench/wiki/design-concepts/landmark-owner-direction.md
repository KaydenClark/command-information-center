# Candidate Landmark: Owner Direction

## What the Landmark Is

Inference: this candidate landmark groups how the Owner identifies worthwhile attention, answers decisions and issues direction through CIC. Its current decision records cover the receiving-system boundary and the separate Priority/Value model. The ability to recommend attention does not establish an implementation for assigning classifications or delivering commands.

This is a provisional grouping, not a confirmed landmark assignment. The room has no configured Landmark Tracker collection; this Design Concept article is the fallback permitted by [CIC Blueprint Teardown — S-000B](../../specs/S-000B-cic-blueprint-teardown/SPEC.md). No native landmark or question-card record is claimed.
