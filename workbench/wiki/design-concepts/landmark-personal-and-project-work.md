# Candidate Landmark: Personal and Project Work

## What the Landmark Is

Inference: this candidate landmark groups the central picture of personal life and project work, including familiar Taskboard interactions. The decision record supports their presence together; it does not settle personal integrations, personal taskboard storage or the meaning of every statistic. The CIC product definition and portable Owner terminology remain at Blueprint level.

This is a provisional grouping, not a confirmed landmark assignment. The room has no configured Landmark Tracker collection; this Design Concept article is the fallback permitted by [CIC Blueprint Teardown — S-000B](../../specs/S-000B-cic-blueprint-teardown/SPEC.md).
