# DDR-000E — CIC opens on a homepage dashboard across projects

## Decision

> CIC opens on a homepage dashboard across projects. It lets the Owner quickly answer:
>
> - What changed since I last checked?
> - What is in progress?
> - What needs my attention?
> - What decisions do I need to make?

## Why

The Owner said: “the CIC is for lots of projects, and I might come looking for info about 3 different projects at once. I should be able to find all of that as fast as I can.”

## Considered and Rejected

The assistant recommendation to resume one selected project was rejected in favor of a homepage dashboard.

## Consequences

The catch-up, activity, unblock and grilling flow works across the portfolio, with access to project detail and other project and website status visible. Exact layout, filters, ranking and refresh behavior remain open.

Blueprint. Candidate landmark membership is provisional and recorded in the exploration synthesis; no landmark classification has been confirmed.
