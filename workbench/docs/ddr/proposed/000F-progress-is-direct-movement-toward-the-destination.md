# DDR-000F — Progress is direct movement toward the destination

## Decision

> Progress means getting closer to the destination, as directly as possible.
>
> - Pointless churn and added bloat do not count as progress.
> - The best path is a straight line toward the intended outcome.
> - A detour is justified when an obstacle cannot be overcome on that path.
>
> For CIC, I’d translate that into updates that explain what brought the project closer to its destination, and identify any detour and the obstacle that required it. Agent activity and completed-task counts provide context; they don’t establish progress by themselves.

## Why

The Owner said: “we should be closer to the destination” and “the best progress is a straight line towards the destination, only deviated by obstacles we could not overcome.”

## Considered and Rejected

Pointless turning or churn and creating bloat were explicitly excluded from progress. Activity and task counts alone do not establish advancement.

## Consequences

Updates explain destination advancement and justified detours. No numerical progress formula, automated judgment or change to a project destination is settled.
